Dragon Bosses

Recommended teams, reasons, and exact strategy for each dragon. Use Team Builder to adapt these to your roster.

Important: Gem luck still matters. These teams are built around the actual boss abilities, not leaderboard screenshots. Color diversity is intentional so you can still make matches under debuffs. Power numbers do not decide fights — composition and status synergies matter more.

Viserion

Pacify · Raid · Brittle · True Damage

Recommended Team (Primary)

Leader: Euron Greyjoy
Arya Stark
Yara Greyjoy (or Theon)
Gregor Clegane (or Ghost)
Laenor Velaryon (flex)

Why this works

  • Golden Scales — Every Raid (especially Euron’s skill Raid) drops Viserion’s DEF & Durability and gives the whole team 30% Stamina.
  • Brittle (Arya / Ghost) — Completely shuts down Rude Awakening II. You can hit him while Pacified with zero punishment.
  • No Birthright / Fury / Enraged for Claw Swipe II to strip.
  • Mixed gem colors so the –50% Gem Damage from Unchained Dragon hurts less.
  • Gregor / Laenor add real damage and secondary Raid without feeding the wrong buffs.

Strategy

  • Never skill him while Pacified unless he is Brittle.
  • Stack Raid as high as possible — this is the main damage multiplier.
  • Do not Reinforce. It feeds Patience II and triggers 100% HP True Damage on the reinforcing unit.
  • Expect every Shield to be destroyed the moment he wakes (Rude Awakening II).
  • Use the 2-turn sleep windows (Own Pace) to set up, then unload when Brittle is up or the window is open.
  • He is immune to Fire and Poison — do not waste those tools.

Drogon

Fire · Speed · High pressure

Recommended Team (Primary)

Leader: Khal Drogo
Daario Naharis
High Daenerys
Nymeria Sand (or Gregor)
Ned Stark (or Duncan)

Why this works

  • Free Cities stamina engine (Drogo lead) lets you dump skills rapidly before Drogon snowballs.
  • High single-target and AoE pressure from Daario / Dany / Nymeria or Gregor.
  • Ned or Duncan provide a durable body so your damage dealers stay alive.
  • Does not rely on Birthright or slow sustain — Drogon rewards speed and real damage.

Strategy

  • Play aggressively. This is a race, not a stall.
  • Keep skills flowing — Free Cities wants constant action.
  • Protect your highest damage dealers; Drogon hits hard and fast.
  • Raid is useful for opening but not mandatory if your Free Cities core is strong.

Rhaegal

Taunt · Bleed · Fire · Grudge · Gem Damage reduction

Key Abilities

  • Unrelenting Assault — Uses Stamina skill every turn if able. 30% ATK Physical + 2 Grudge, then +20% ATK per Grudge.
  • Lasting Agony — 100% ATK Physical single-target bite.
  • Fuel to the Flames II — Skill while Bleeding = 2% Max HP True Damage per Bleed. Start of turn: 20k flat True Damage per Fire mark. When the Taunting ally is Healed they gain Block + Renew; if Targaryen or Bolton, Rhaegal also takes 20% Max HP Physical.
  • Wrathful Gaze — At start applies indefinite Taunt to the strongest enemy (then next strongest). Only Rhaegal can grant this Taunt. While Taunting, that champion gains +40% Stamina every turn start.
  • High and Mighty II — Immune to Stun / Pacified / Deceived. Whole team –50% Gem Damage. Reinforce = 100% HP Pool True Damage to the reinforcing unit.
  • Opening Salvo II — Each turn start eats all Birthright, Fury, and Shields (replaces with Grudge). Also eats Ice/Brittle on himself and deals 100% ATK Fire to all enemies per Ice eaten.

What actually scores (read this first)

Official tips still apply: he skills every turn → Bleed does heavy True Damage, Fire deals bonus damage, keep the Taunting ally alive. But pure theory Bleed packages can underperform on real boards. Top Tips & Tricks scores lean on high-investment pieces (often dual Daenerys Targaryen (Blue) / Targ cores). Alliance tests have also scored with Free Folk + Greyjoy pressure. Use a real Taunter; do not Reinforce; avoid Birthright/Fury/Shield walls.

Recommended Teams (pick based on what you have)

Team A — High-investment Targ / Blue Dany (leaderboard style):
Strong Taunt (Brienne / Sandor / Areo Hotah) · Daenerys Targaryen (Blue) · second high Dany or Daemon · Oberyn or Fire damage · Flex damage
Why: Matches top Tips & Tricks boards. Blue Dany + invested partners carry damage while Taunt soaks Wrathful Gaze. Keep Taunter alive.
Team B — Free Folk / Greyjoy pressure (alliance tested):
Ygritte (or strong Free Folk lead) · Euron · Davos · Osha / Tormund · Theon or second Greyjoy
Why: Scored ~5.5k in alliance testing when a pure Bleed package only managed ~2.3k on a similar board. Raid + board control + Free Folk crit package can outpace theory Bleed if gems favor it.
Team C — Classic Bleed / Fire grind:
Brienne or Areo Hotah (Taunt lead) · Oberyn Martell · Olenna Tyrell · Nymeria / Gregor · Daenerys Targaryen (Blue) or heal
Why: Maximizes Fuel to the Flames True Damage. Works when you can keep Bleed up and the Taunter alive. If this shape underperforms for you, switch to A or B.
Team D — Free Cities + protected Taunt:
Drogo · Daario · Daenerys Targaryen (Blue) · Brienne / Sandor / Areo · Oberyn or Nymeria
Why: Stamina pressure + real damage while still answering Wrathful Gaze. Prefer this if your Bleed tools are under-invested.

Always: protect the Taunter Rhaegal forces onto you. Never Reinforce. Do not build Birthright/Fury/Shield walls (Opening Salvo eats them). Label same-name champs by color — use Daenerys Targaryen (Blue).

Why this works

  • Rhaegal forces Taunt onto your strongest unit and only he can grant it — bring a real Taunter you are willing to let him target and keep alive.
  • Bleed and Fire trigger Fuel to the Flames II (True Damage on him every turn and when he skills).
  • Opening Salvo eats Birthright / Fury / Shields every turn — avoid relying on those buffs.
  • –50% Gem Damage (High and Mighty) means mixed colors and skill damage matter more than pure gem matching.
  • Healing the Taunter also gives Block + Renew and can punish Rhaegal if the Taunter is Targaryen or Bolton.

Strategy

  • Protect the Taunter at all costs. If he dies early the fight becomes chaotic and Stamina advantage is lost.
  • Apply Bleed and Fire and keep them up — this is the main damage engine against him.
  • Do not Reinforce. High and Mighty punishes it with massive True Damage.
  • Do not lean on Birthright, Fury, or big Shield walls — Opening Salvo eats them every turn and turns them into Grudge.
  • Skill consistently. He skills every turn; matching that tempo with Bleed/Fire pressure wins long fights.
  • He is immune to Stun, Pacified, and Deceived — do not waste those tools.

Icy Viserion

Ice · Reinforce pressure · Speed race

Recommended Teams (Night King on all three)

Team A — Night King controlled Ice (primary):
The Night King (Leader) · Ice Warden · Bran Stark · Arya Stark · Ned Stark
Why: Night King is the best-fit lead. Ice Warden / Bran control the pace. Arya and Ned protect the package. Keep Night King alive.
Team B — Night King + Free Cities hybrid:
The Night King (Leader) · Khal Drogo · Daario Naharis · Nymeria Sand · Ice Warden
Why: Same Night King core with speed damage to end the fight before Ice stacks become oppressive. Ice Warden is the defensive body.
Team C — Night King + Stark wall:
The Night King (Leader) · Ned Stark · Bran Stark · Arya Stark · Sandor Clegane
Why: Maximum defensive posture when the board is hostile. Reinforce only when the timing is clean. Keep Night King alive at all costs.

Why this works

  • Night King is the control lead for this fight — do not default Drogo just because he is strong elsewhere.
  • A defensive body (Ice Warden / Ned / Sandor) keeps Night King online so Ice / Reinforce control stays available.
  • The hybrid version still uses Drogo + Blue-Dany-tier pressure when you can race, but Night King stays Leader.

Strategy

  • Do not mindlessly Reinforce — it can backfire. Time it.
  • Keep Night King alive. If he dies the Ice package collapses.
  • If the board is open, use the hybrid to finish before Ice stacks take over.
  • If the board is hostile, sit on the Stark wall and outlast.

Four Proven Engines

These packages work across many fights. Use them as building blocks for dragons, raids, and war.

1. Meryn Control Core (Hold / Defense)

Shape: Meryn Trant (Leader) + Jaqen / Olenna + Tyrion + Sandor / Ned + sustain

Why: Meryn punishes every enemy skill with Stun + Wound. Extremely hard to break cleanly. Ideal for holding outposts or defense.

How: Keep Meryn alive. Let the opponent skill into the punish loop. Do not chase kills — win by attrition and forced bad decisions.

2. Free Cities Speed (Primary Offense)

Shape: Khal Drogo (Leader) + Daario + high Daenerys + Nymeria / Gregor + flex

Why: Highest-ceiling pure attack engine. Stamina loop + Wrath / crit pressure ends fights before the enemy stabilizes.

How: Race. Open hard, keep skills flowing, protect the damage dealers. Do not stall.

3. Scout + Gem Crit (Snowball)

Shape: Varys (Leader) + Davos + Drogo / Stannis + high Dany + flex

Why: Varys Scouts at start and grants Stealth when allies drop low. When a Scouted enemy takes Skill damage the team gets +25% Gem Crit Chance. Davos adds more Scout + Seeker gems. Can snowball hard when the board cooperates.

How: Land Scout early, trigger Crit windows with skills, use Dany Wrath or red engines as the finisher. Board-dependent — if gems hate you the plan slows down.

4. Raid + Brittle (Viserion & skill-Raid fights)

Shape: Euron Greyjoy (Leader) + Arya Stark + Yara / Theon + Gregor / Ghost + flex

Why: Skill Raid maximizes Golden Scales (DEF drop + team Stamina). Brittle completely shuts down Rude Awakening punishment. Mixed colors help under Gem Damage debuffs.

How: Never skill while Pacified unless Brittle is up. Stack Raid. Do not Reinforce. Use sleep windows to set up big turns.

War Overview

Stages

  • Stage 2 — Teams lock in.
  • Stage 3 — Outpost placement fights. You place teams on specific outposts; the opponent does the same. You fight whoever is on that outpost. Outpost bonuses apply to the fight.
  • Stage 4 — Final war. Any outposts you won in Stage 3 carry their bonuses forward. You face strong opposing teams and choose Easy / Med / Hard (Hard pays more points but is riskier).

Key principles

  • Composition and status synergies matter more than raw power numbers.
  • Match your team identity to the current outpost bonus when possible (faction ATK, DEF, Tenacity, Unnatural Resistance, etc.). Bonuses change every war.
  • Defense is harder than offense because the attacker controls the gem board.
  • Do not force under-leveled champions just to claim a faction bonus — the power gap is usually not worth it.
  • Gem color diversity helps under –50% Gem Damage effects and general board control fights.

Default four war jobs: Lannister core · Free Cities speed · Baratheon camp / Scout · Meryn hold. Chase the outpost faction bonus only when those champions are actually strong.

Raid Attack (Offense)

Primary: Free Cities — Drogo (Leader) + Blue Dany + Daario + Jorah + Nymeria

Why: Highest-ceiling stamina race. Alternative: dual Dany + Sandor/Jorah, or Tywin/Euron into a Lannister wall.

Raid Defense

Primary: Meryn + Sandor + Tywin/Cersei + Olenna + Joffrey

Why: Attacker owns the gems, so defense wins with Stun/Wound punish — not a race. Alt: Olenna + Nymeria + Gregor wall.
