Dragon Bosses

Each dragon fights differently. Use Team Builder for roster-based picks, then adjust with the notes below.

Important: Leaderboard scores are a starting point. Gem luck matters. Status tools (Raid, Ice→Brittle, Fire, Wound) often matter more than pure faction.

Viserion

Ice · Pacify · Raid pressure

Do: Bring Raid (Euron). Stack pressure. Prefer Brittle / Ice tools. Don’t hit while Pacified. Don’t lean on pure Shield walls (Wake destroys them).

Starter: Raid leader · Damage · Damage · Control · Flex

Drogon

Fire · Speed · Real damage

Do: Favor Damage and pressure. Raid helps open. Fire / Fury lines when invested (Daemon + Caraxes when both are available — multi-dragon is Daemon’s strength).

Starter: Damage · Raid/Pressure · Damage · Utility · Flex

Rhaegal

Bleed · Fire · Protected Taunt

Do: Protect a real Taunt (Brienne, Sandor, Jorah). Bleed / Fire / Poison tools (Oberyn, Olenna) help grind.

Starter: Taunt · Damage · Sustain · Pressure · Flex

Icy Viserion

Reinforce · Ice · Careful engines

Do: Ice / Reinforce / control (Night King, Bran). Don’t blindly stack pure Reinforce and feed the boss. Pair with real damage.

Starter: Reinforce · Control · Damage · Support · Flex

Roster note: Mark what you own in My Roster, then run Team Builder. Use the tools you have — Taunt, Raid, control, damage — based on your roster.
