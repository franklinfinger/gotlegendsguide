Dragon Bosses

Each dragon fights differently. Use Team Builder for roster-based picks, then adjust with the notes below.

Important: Leaderboard screenshots show high scores, but gem luck matters. Treat top-score teams as a starting point — not the only answer. Status tools (Raid, Ice→Brittle, Fire, Wound) often matter more than pure faction.

Viserion

Ice · Pacify · Raid pressure fight

What he does: Pacifies, punishes bad timing, and rewards Raid stacks. Immune to Fire and Poison in many stages. Wake can erase Shields.

Do:

  • Bring Raid (Euron is the default skill-Raid leader)
  • Stack pressure once openings appear
  • Prefer Brittle / Ice tools when you have them
  • Control the fight — don’t spam into Pacify

Don’t:

  • Hit while Pacified
  • Lean on pure Shield walls (Wake destroys them)
  • Feed endless Reinforces that accelerate his Patience

Starter shape: Raid leader · Damage · Damage · Control · Flex

Use Team Builder → Viserion for a roster-based line.

Drogon

Fire · Speed · Real damage

What he does: Fast, aggressive, Fire-heavy. Shields are less reliable; you need actual damage output and tempo.

Do:

  • Favor Damage and pressure over pure tanks
  • Bring Raid if available to open the fight
  • Use Dragon / Fire / Fury lines when invested (Daemon, Caraxes, Rhaenyra, Rhaenys)

Don’t:

  • Build a full Taunt-and-hide team and expect it to win by stalling alone

Starter shape: Damage · Raid/Pressure · Damage · Utility · Flex

Use Team Builder → Drogon.

Rhaegal

Bleed · Fire · Protected Taunt

What he does: Rewards consistent skill use and sustained pressure. Bleed and Fire help. A protected Taunter often stabilizes the fight.

Do:

  • Protect a real Taunt (Brienne, Sandor, Jorah, Duncan)
  • Skill every turn when the fight rewards it
  • Bleed / Fire / Poison tools (Oberyn, Olenna, Fire dragons) help grind him down

Starter shape: Taunt · Damage · Sustain · Pressure · Flex

Use Team Builder → Rhaegal.

Icy Viserion

Reinforce · Ice · Careful engines

What he does: Punishes mindless Reinforce spam while still wanting Ice / control tools. Night King lines are strong when used carefully.

Do:

  • Lean Ice / Reinforce / control lines (Night King, Bran, Ice tools)
  • Control skill cadence — don’t auto-pilot
  • Pair with real damage so the fight ends

Don’t:

  • Blindly stack pure Reinforce engines and feed the boss

Starter shape: Reinforce · Reinforce/Control · Damage · Support · Flex

Use Team Builder → Icy Viserion. Night King Ally gem (Awakened Horror) is relevant here.

Roster note: If your alliance has mixed investment levels, mark only what each player owns in My Roster, then run Team Builder. Stronger players can field full Raid + Dragon lines; others should prioritize Taunt + one clear damage threat + whatever control they have.
