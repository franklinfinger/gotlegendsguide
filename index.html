Role glossary
  • • Raid — stacks that weaken the enemy (skill-based preferred)
  • • Taunt — forces the enemy to hit the right target
  • • Sustain — heals, shields, durability
  • • Control — stun, strip, pacify, reinforce, ice, etc.
  • • Damage — finishers once openings exist
  • • Leader — champion whose leader skill is active

War 4 Non-Overlapping Teams

Build from your roster: Team Builder → Alliance War makes 4 teams with no duplicate champions.

5-minute War builder (manual)
  1. List your strongest available champions
  2. Best leader + 4 partners → Team 1 (often Lannister or control core)
  3. Taunt + sustain → Team 2
  4. Raid + damage → Team 3
  5. Leftovers with a purpose → Team 4

Tips playbook

• Spread Raid and Taunt across War teams — don’t put every key tool on Team 1.

• Four complete teams beat three perfect ones and one empty shell.

• Faction bonuses are strong, but status interactions (Ice→Brittle, Fury→Enraged, Wound shutting heals) often decide fights.

• Ally gems are free value when both partners are available — check the Allies page.

• Builder suggestions are role-based starting points. Adjust for stars, gear, and what you know works in our alliance.

• Power numbers do not decide fights. Composition and status synergies matter more than total might.
