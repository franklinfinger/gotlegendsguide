Raids Attack & Defense

Focus on strong Raid application, control, and sustain.

Raid Attack

Goal: Apply Raid stacks quickly, then convert into damage.

  • • Prioritize champions that apply Raid from skills
  • • Pair them with high single-target damage
  • • Control (stun / pacify / strip) keeps the fight clean
  • • Avoid pure shield walls — many bosses punish them
  • • Fury, Enraged, and critical pressure are strong partners
  • • Keep pressure up after the first Raid stacks land

Raid Defense

Goal: Survive the first turns and punish over-aggression.

  • • Strong Taunt + sustain is the foundation
  • • Counter-attack and shield-break value is high
  • • Brienne-style and Corlys-style kits perform well
  • • A cleanser or buffer helps a lot
  • • Durability > pure burst on defense
  • • Teams that still function after one KO are valuable
Attack vs Defense at a glance
  • Attack: Raid first, damage second, control third
  • Defense: Taunt first, sustain second, punish tools third
  • Same champions can appear in either role depending on the rest of the team
Example skeletons (fill with your best available):
  • • Attack: Raid applier · Damage · Damage/Control · Utility · Support
  • • Defense: Taunt · Sustain · Counter/Control · Flex damage · Buffer/Cleanse

These are role skeletons, not fixed names.

Sample directions (example names only):
  • • Attack: Euron-style Raid · Daemon / dragon damage · Jon or Ned utility · flexible support/control
  • • Defense: Brienne-style Taunt · Corlys-style sustain · control/counter · flex damage · buffer if available

See Champions for role groupings.

Priority order: 1) Reliable Raid → 2) Real damage → 3) Control or sustain.
Common mistakes:
  • Stacking too many Shields against bosses that erase them
  • Having Raid but no real damage to finish
  • Putting every key tool on one team
  • Ignoring leader skills
Attack checklist:
  • Consistent Raid applier?
  • Real damage once Raid is up?
  • Any control or strip?
  • Over-relying on Shields?
War 4 Non-Overlapping Teams

Build four strong, non-overlapping teams. Outpost bonuses help, but solid team strength matters most.

TEAM 1
Primary Offense

Strongest synergy. Best damage + best support. The team you trust in hard fights.

TEAM 2
Control / Sustain

Taunt + durability. Good for holding or absorbing pressure.

TEAM 3
Raid / Burst

Heavy Raid + burst. Strong into defensive setups.

TEAM 4
Flex / Specialist

Remaining strong pieces. Can lean toward an outpost bonus if needed.

Building order:
  1. Strongest 5 → Team 1
  2. Next best sustain / Taunt core → Team 2
  3. Remaining Raid + damage → Team 3
  4. Leftover strong pieces → Team 4
What good War teams look like:
  • Team 1: Clear win condition + enough support to execute it
  • Team 2: Hard to kill, forces the enemy to work for every KO
  • Team 3: Raid + damage that can crack defensive walls
  • Team 4: Still a real team, not just leftovers with no synergy
Placement note: When leadership assigns outposts, put your strongest teams where they ask first. Multiple teams can go into the same outpost if that is the plan. Outpost bonuses are helpful, but team quality comes first.
If you’re short on legendaries: Still aim for four complete teams. Use epics/commons to fill Taunt, cleanse, or specific debuffs.
Practical tips:
  • Field the four strongest teams you can.
  • Spread Taunt and Raid across teams.
  • Four complete teams beat three strong ones and one empty shell.
  • Strong leader skills belong in the leader slot.
  • Don’t weaken a team just to chase a small outpost bonus.
War checklist:
  • All four teams complete?
  • Taunt / sustain spread?
  • Raid on more than one team?
  • Each team has a clear purpose?
  • Best leaders in leader slots?
Dragons Boss Counters
V

Viserion – Growing Terror

Most detailed boss kit currently available
Critical Rules
  • • Do not hit while Pacified
  • • Brittle = safe to hit while sleeping
  • • Every Raid stack weakens him
  • • On wake: destroys Shields + refills Stamina
  • • Immune to Fire and Poison
  • • Patience meter → True Damage when full
  • • Reinforces accelerate the meter and get punished
What to Bring
  • • Reliable Raid (especially from Skills)
  • • Ways to apply Brittle
  • • High single-target pressure
  • • Avoid pure Shield walls
  • • Be careful with frequent Reinforces

Simple approach: Bring Raid, bring damage, respect Pacified, don’t over-rely on Shields.

Drogon

  • • Fast champs stop Brimstone Rain
  • • Birthright is strong
  • • Immune to Fire
  • • Can destroy Shields
  • • Speed & pressure > pure tankiness

Rhaegal

  • • Skill every turn → Bleed excellent
  • • Fire deals bonus damage
  • • Keep Taunter alive
  • • Consistent skill pressure is rewarded

Icy Viserion

  • • Reinforce champions punish him
  • • Strong gem teams help
  • • Frequent Skills trigger Icebound sooner
  • • Immune to most Debuffs
Quick dragon matching:
  • Viserion: Raid + Brittle + single-target, light on Shields/Reinforce
  • Drogon: Speed + pressure, Birthright if you have it
  • Rhaegal: Bleed + Fire + protect your Taunter
  • Icy Viserion: Reinforce value + skill cadence control
Across all dragons: Raid + real damage + not over-committing to Shields covers a lot.
Dragon prep checklist:
  • Which dragon am I fighting?
  • Do I have Raid (or the specific tool that dragon cares about)?
  • Am I bringing real damage, not just survival?
  • Am I over-relying on Shields?
