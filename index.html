Dragons Boss Counters

Viserion – Growing Terror

  • • Do not hit while Pacified
  • • Brittle = safe while sleeping
  • • Every Raid stack helps
  • • Wake destroys Shields + refills Stamina
  • • Immune to Fire and Poison
  • • Reinforces accelerate Patience meter

Sample

  1. Leader: Euron (Raid)
  2. Daemon / dragon damage
  3. Brittle tool if available
  4. Jon or Ned
  5. Flex damage

Drogon

Speed + pressure. Birthright strong. Can erase Shields.

Sample direction: Fast leader · Raid · real damage · skip pure tanks

Rhaegal

Bleed + Fire. Protect Taunter. Skill every turn rewarded.

Sample direction: Taunt leader · Bleed/Fire · skill pressure

Icy Viserion

Reinforce punishes him. Control skill cadence.

Sample: Night King leader · Ice Warden · Bran · damage · flex

Quick match: Viserion → Raid+Brittle · Drogon → speed · Rhaegal → Bleed/Fire · Icy → Reinforce
Faction cores When your roster leans one way

These are building blocks, not forced full teams. Use them when that faction is your highest investment.

LANNISTER

Tywin · Gregor · Cersei · Tyrion · Sandor / Jaime / Jaqen

Strong offense or durable defense. Add Raid from outside if needed.

GREYJOY / FLEET

Euron · Yara · Corlys · Theon · Laenor flex

Natural Raid / pressure core for Attack and War Team 3.

BLACKS / DRAGONS

Daemon · Rhaenyra · Caraxes · Meleys · Laenor / Corlys

High single-target and fire pressure.

NIGHT / REINFORCE

Night King · Ice Warden · Bran · Craster · Thoros-style flex

War flex and Icy Viserion. Careful on standard Viserion.

STARK / UTILITY

Jon · Ned · Arya · Bran · Brienne flex

Clean control and flexible damage across teams.

TAUNT HOLD

Brienne · Corlys · Sandor · Meryn · buffer/cleanse

Raid Defense and War Team 2 backbone.

Notes & FAQ

• Built for Old Peeps on Porches.

• Missing a name? Same role on Champions = valid swap.

• Short on legendaries? Still field four complete War teams. Epics fill Taunt, cleanse, and Team 4.

• Two sample sets conflict? Pick one set. Never reuse a champion across your four War teams.

• Portraits: Stylized initials only — account screenshots stay private.

• When in doubt: Raid + damage + keep the team standing.
