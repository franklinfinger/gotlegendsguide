Raids Attack & Defense

Raid Attack A — Raid pressure

Default attack sample

  1. Leader: Euron Greyjoy
  2. Daemon Targaryen
  3. Dragon champion (damage)
  4. Jon Snow
  5. Ned Stark

Swaps: any Raid applier · any high damage · any control

Raid Attack B — Lannister lean

If your Lannisters are your best built

  1. Leader: Tywin Lannister
  2. Gregor Clegane
  3. Cersei Lannister
  4. Tyrion Lannister
  5. Sandor / Jaime / Jaqen flex

Still add Raid from somewhere if the boss needs it

Raid Defense A — Taunt hold

Default defense sample

  1. Leader: Brienne of Tarth
  2. Corlys Velaryon
  3. Night King
  4. Jon Snow / Sandor
  5. Flex damage or buffer

Raid Defense B — Reinforce hold

If Night King / Ice line is strong

  1. Leader: Night King
  2. Ice Warden
  3. Brienne or other Taunt
  4. Bran / Craster
  5. Flex damage

Watch Reinforce use into Viserion boss

Attack vs Defense: Attack = Raid → damage → control. Defense = Taunt → sustain → punish tools.
Checklist: Raid applier? Real damage? Control or sustain? Not over-reliant on Shields?

War checklist: 4 complete teams · Taunt/Raid spread · clear purpose · leaders in leader slots
Dragons Boss Counters

Viserion – Growing Terror

  • • Do not hit while Pacified
  • • Brittle = safe while sleeping
  • • Every Raid stack helps
  • • Wake destroys Shields + refills Stamina
  • • Immune to Fire and Poison
  • • Reinforces accelerate Patience meter

Sample team

  1. Leader: Euron (Raid)
  2. Daemon / dragon damage
  3. Brittle tool if available
  4. Jon or Ned
  5. Flex damage

Drogon

Speed + pressure. Birthright strong. Can erase Shields.

Leader idea: best speed/pressure piece + Raid + real damage

Rhaegal

Bleed + Fire. Protect Taunter. Skill every turn rewarded.

Leader idea: Taunt/sustain + Bleed/Fire pressure

Icy Viserion

Reinforce punishes him. Control skill cadence.

Leader idea: Night King + Reinforce support

Across dragons: Raid + real damage + not over-committing to Shields covers most of the basics.
