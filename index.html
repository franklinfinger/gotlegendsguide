Raids

Focus on strong Raid application, control, and sustain.

Raid Attack

Goal: Apply Raid stacks quickly, then convert into damage.

  • • Prioritize champions that apply Raid from skills
  • • Pair with high single-target damage dealers
  • • Control (stun / pacify / strip) helps keep the fight clean
  • • Avoid pure shield walls — many bosses punish them

Raid Defense

Goal: Survive the first few turns and punish over-aggression.

  • • Strong Taunt + sustain is the foundation
  • • Counter-attack and shield-break value is high
  • • Brienne-style and Corlys-style kits perform well
  • • Keep at least one reliable cleanser / buffer if possible

War – 4 Teams

Build four strong, non-overlapping teams. Outpost bonuses are helpful but secondary to raw team quality for most of the alliance.

TEAM 1
Primary Offense

Your highest power + best synergy combination. This is the team you trust to win hard fights.

TEAM 2
Control / Sustain

Taunt + durability focused. Good for holding outposts or absorbing pressure.

TEAM 3
Raid / Burst

Heavy Raid application and burst damage. Strong into many defensive setups.

TEAM 4
Flex / Specialist

Remaining strong pieces. Can be tuned toward a specific outpost bonus if needed.

Note for the alliance: Most members should simply field their four strongest possible teams. Only the highest-power players need to stress over perfect outpost matching.

Dragon Counters

V

Viserion – Growing Terror

Most detailed boss kit currently available
Critical Rules
  • • Do not hit while he is Pacified — he gets angry
  • • If he is Brittle, you can safely hit him while sleeping
  • • Every stack of Raid significantly weakens his defenses
  • • When he wakes he destroys all Shields and refills Stamina
  • • Immune to Fire and Poison
  • • Patience meter → True Damage based on max HP when full
  • • Reinforce enemies get punished hard and accelerate his meter
What to Bring
  • • Reliable Raid application (especially from Skills)
  • • Ways to apply Brittle
  • • High single-target pressure
  • • Avoid leaning purely on Shields
  • • Be careful with frequent Reinforces
Top-team pattern observed

Highest scores lean into Raid + control + strong damage. Exact champion lists will be refined as more of the roster is locked in.

Drogon

  • • Use fast champions to stop Brimstone Rain
  • • Birthright is strong against him
  • • Immune to Fire
  • • Can destroy Shields

Rhaegal

  • • Uses skill every turn → Bleed is excellent
  • • Fire deals bonus damage
  • • Keep your Taunting ally alive

Icy Viserion

  • • Reinforce champions punish him
  • • Strong gem teams exploit Undead Dragon
  • • Frequent Skills trigger Icebound sooner
  • • Immune to most Debuffs
