Core principles

  • • Spread Taunt and Raid across teams.
  • • Leaders matter — put strong leader skills in the leader slot.
  • • Four complete teams beat three perfect ones and one empty shell.
Raids Attack & Defense

Sample — Raid Attack

Leader first · swap freely

  1. Leader: Euron Greyjoy (Raid)
  2. Daemon Targaryen (damage)
  3. Dragon champion (damage / pressure)
  4. Jon Snow (utility)
  5. Ned Stark (control)

Swaps: Any Raid applier · any high damage · any clean control. See Champions page.

Sample — Raid Defense

Leader first · swap freely

  1. Leader: Brienne of Tarth (Taunt)
  2. Corlys Velaryon (sustain)
  3. Night King (control)
  4. Jon Snow / Sandor (utility / protect)
  5. Flex damage or buffer

Swaps: Any Taunt tank · durable support · control piece.

Priority: Reliable Raid → real damage → control or sustain. Avoid pure Shield walls on many bosses.
War 4 Non-Overlapping Teams

Four samples with no shared champions. Swap within roles as needed.

  1. Leader: Daemon Targaryen
  2. Caraxes / dragon damage
  3. Jon Snow
  4. Utility / support flex
  5. Second damage or control
TEAM 2 — CONTROL / SUSTAIN
  1. Leader: Brienne of Tarth
  2. Corlys Velaryon
  3. Ned Stark
  4. Flex sustain / control
  5. Flex damage
TEAM 3 — RAID / BURST
  1. Leader: Euron Greyjoy
  2. Second damage dealer
  3. Control / strip
  4. Pressure flex (Yara / dragon)
  5. Support or secondary Raid
TEAM 4 — FLEX
  1. Leader: Night King (or best remaining)
  2. Mance Rayder
  3. Ice Warden / Bran if available
  4. Remaining strong pieces
  5. Epic fillers as needed
Placement: Follow alliance leadership on outposts. Team quality first, bonuses second.
Dragons Boss Counters

Viserion – sample

  1. Leader: Euron Greyjoy (Raid)
  2. Daemon or dragon damage
  3. Brittle applicator if available
  4. Jon / Ned utility
  5. Flex damage / control

Do not hit while Pacified. Brittle = safe while sleeping. Light on Shields / Reinforce.

Drogon

Speed + pressure. Birthright if you have it. Skip pure tanks.

Rhaegal

Bleed + Fire. Protect your Taunter. Skill-every-turn pressure.

Icy Viserion

Reinforce value (Night King line). Control skill cadence.
