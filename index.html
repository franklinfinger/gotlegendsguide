Quick start
  1. Strongest 5 → offense
  2. Taunt + sustain → hold
  3. Raid + damage → burst
  4. Leftovers with a purpose → flex
  5. Dragons: Raid first, then boss tools
Before you lock teams — 30-second checklist
  • Do at least two teams have Raid or real pressure?
  • Is Taunt / sustain on more than one team?
  • Is every team complete (5 champions with a job)?
  • Are best leaders in leader slots?
  • Did you avoid stacking every key tool on Team 1 only?

If the enemy defense looks like…
  • Heavy Taunt / sustain → bring Raid + burst (Attack A or C)
  • Pure Shield wall → avoid stacking more Shields; bring strip / pressure
  • Glass cannon offense → Defense A or C to outlast and punish
  • Reinforce engine → control timing; don’t feed free value
Priority: Reliable Raid → real damage → control or sustain.

Where to place teams
  • Follow alliance leadership first
  • Put your strongest team where the fight is hardest
  • Hold teams are good on contested or defensive outposts
  • Raid/burst teams are good into known tanky defenses
  • Don’t gut a team just for a small outpost bonus
Dragons Boss Counters

Viserion – Growing Terror

  • • Do not hit while Pacified
  • • Brittle = safe while sleeping
  • • Every Raid stack helps
  • • Wake destroys Shields + refills Stamina
  • • Immune to Fire and Poison
  • • Reinforces accelerate Patience meter

Sample A

  1. Leader: Euron
  2. Daemon / dragon damage
  3. Brittle tool
  4. Jon or Ned
  5. Flex

Sample B (if no Euron)

Any skill Raid · two damage · control · sustain flex

Drogon

Speed + pressure. Birthright strong. Shields can be erased.

Bring: fast champs · Raid · real damage · skip pure tanks

Rhaegal

Bleed + Fire. Protect Taunter. Skill every turn rewarded.

Bring: Taunt · Bleed/Fire · skill pressure

Icy Viserion

Reinforce punishes him. Control skill cadence.

Sample: Night King · Ice Warden · Bran · damage · flex

Quick match: Viserion → Raid+Brittle · Drogon → speed · Rhaegal → Bleed/Fire · Icy → Reinforce
