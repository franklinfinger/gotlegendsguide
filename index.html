Role glossary
  • • Raid — stacks that weaken the enemy (skill-based preferred)
  • • Taunt — forces the enemy to hit the right target
  • • Sustain — heals, shields, durability
  • • Control — stun, strip, pacify, reinforce, ice, etc.
  • • Damage — finishers once openings exist
  • • Leader — champion whose leader skill is active

Tips playbook

• Spread Raid and Taunt across War teams — don’t stack every key tool on Team 1.

• Four complete teams beat three perfect ones and one empty shell.

• Faction bonuses are strong, but status interactions (Ice→Brittle, Fury→Enraged, Wound shutting heals) often win harder content.

• Ally gems are free value when both partners are available — check the Allies page.

• Builder suggestions are role-based starting points. Adjust for stars, gear, and what you know works.
