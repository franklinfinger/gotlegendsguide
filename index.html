Core team-building principles
  • • Roles first, names second. You need Raid, damage, and some form of sustain or control. The exact champions matter less than covering those jobs.
  • • Spread key tools. Do not put every Taunt and every Raid applier on the same team if you need four teams for War.
  • • Leaders matter. A strong leader skill can lift an entire team. Put your best leaders in the leader slot when possible.
  • • Complete teams beat incomplete theory. Four full teams are better than three perfect ones and one weak shell.

Raids

Focus on strong Raid application, control, and sustain. The same principles apply whether you are attacking or defending.

Raid Attack

Goal: Apply Raid stacks quickly, then convert into damage.

  • • Prioritize champions that apply Raid from skills
  • • Pair them with high single-target damage dealers
  • • Control (stun / pacify / strip) keeps the fight clean
  • • Avoid pure shield walls — many bosses punish them
  • • Kits that also bring Fury, Enraged, or critical pressure are strong partners
  • • Look for ways to keep pressure up after the first Raid stacks land

Raid Defense

Goal: Survive the first few turns and punish over-aggression.

  • • Strong Taunt + sustain is the foundation
  • • Counter-attack and shield-break value is high
  • • Brienne-style and Corlys-style kits perform well here
  • • Keep at least one reliable cleanser or buffer if possible
  • • Durability and recovery matter more than pure burst on defense
  • • A team that can still function after losing one member is very valuable
Quick priority order for most players: 1) Reliable Raid application → 2) A real damage threat → 3) Some form of control or sustain. You do not need a perfect five-man theory team. Strong pieces in the right roles already put you ahead.
Useful role examples from the roster we have so far:
  • • Taunt / Sustain: Brienne of Tarth, Corlys Velaryon
  • • Raid / Pressure: Euron Greyjoy, certain dragon champions
  • • Control / Utility: Night King (reinforce + ice pressure), Ned Stark, Jon Snow
  • • Damage: Adolescent dragons, Daemon, high-attack legendaries

These are role examples, not a forced team list. Use whatever strong versions of these roles you actually own.

Attack checklist:
  • Do I have at least one consistent Raid applier?
  • Do I have real damage once Raid is up?
  • Do I have any control or strip to keep the fight clean?
  • Am I relying too heavily on Shields that the boss will just erase?

War – 4 Teams

Build four strong, non-overlapping teams. Outpost bonuses are helpful, but solid team strength matters most.

TEAM 1
Primary Offense

Your strongest synergy combination. This is the team you trust in hard fights.

Usually your best damage + best support pieces together.

TEAM 2
Control / Sustain

Taunt + durability focused. Good for holding outposts or absorbing pressure.

Think Brienne / Corlys style kits and anyone who keeps the team alive.

TEAM 3
Raid / Burst

Heavy Raid application and burst damage. Strong into many defensive setups.

Put your best Raid appliers here with enough damage to finish fights.

TEAM 4
Flex / Specialist

Remaining strong pieces. Can be tuned toward a specific outpost bonus if needed.

This is where leftover strong champions still form a real team.

Practical tips:

  • Field the four strongest teams you can put together. Outpost matching is a nice bonus, not a requirement.
  • When possible, avoid putting your only Taunt or only Raid appliers on the same team so you keep options across all four.
  • If you are short on top champions, still aim for four complete teams rather than three strong ones and one empty shell.
  • Leader traits matter. If a champion has a strong team-wide leader skill, consider them for the leader slot on one of the four teams.
Simple building order:
  1. Pick your absolute strongest 5 for Team 1
  2. Build the next best sustain / Taunt core for Team 2
  3. Put remaining Raid + damage into Team 3
  4. Fill Team 4 with whatever strong leftover pieces you still have
War checklist before you lock teams:
  • Are all four teams complete (no empty slots if you can avoid it)?
  • Is Taunt / sustain spread instead of stacked on one team?
  • Is Raid application present on more than one team?
  • Does each team still have a clear purpose?

Dragon Counters

V

Viserion – Growing Terror

Most detailed boss kit currently available
Critical Rules
  • • Do not hit while he is Pacified — he gets angry
  • • If he is Brittle, you can safely hit him while sleeping
  • • Every stack of Raid significantly weakens his defenses
  • • When he wakes he destroys all Shields and refills Stamina
  • • Immune to Fire and Poison
  • • Patience meter → True Damage based on max HP when full
  • • Reinforce enemies get punished hard and accelerate his meter
What to Bring
  • • Reliable Raid application (especially from Skills)
  • • Ways to apply Brittle
  • • High single-target pressure
  • • Avoid leaning purely on Shields
  • • Be careful with frequent Reinforces
Observed pattern

Strong results lean into Raid + control + solid damage. Exact champion lists will be refined as more of the roster is locked in.

Simple approach: Bring Raid, bring damage, respect Pacified, and do not over-rely on Shields. That already covers the most important parts of the fight.

Drogon

  • • Use fast champions to stop Brimstone Rain
  • • Birthright is strong against him
  • • Immune to Fire
  • • Can destroy Shields
  • • Speed and pressure matter more than pure tankiness

Rhaegal

  • • Uses skill every turn → Bleed is excellent
  • • Fire deals bonus damage
  • • Keep your Taunting ally alive
  • • Consistent skill pressure is rewarded

Icy Viserion

  • • Reinforce champions punish him
  • • Strong gem teams exploit Undead Dragon
  • • Frequent Skills trigger Icebound sooner
  • • Immune to most Debuffs
Across all dragons: Raid application, real damage, and not over-committing to pure Shields will take you a long way. Adjust the details (speed, Bleed, Reinforce, Brittle) once you know which dragon you are fighting.
Dragon prep checklist:
  • Do I know which dragon I am fighting?
  • Do I have Raid (or the specific tool that dragon cares about)?
  • Am I bringing real damage, not just survival?
  • Am I over-relying on Shields that the boss can erase?
