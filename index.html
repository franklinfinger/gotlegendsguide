Role glossary
  • • Raid — stacks that weaken the enemy (skill-based preferred)
  • • Taunt — forces the enemy to hit the right target
  • • Sustain — heals, shields, durability
  • • Control — stun, strip, pacify, reinforce, ice, etc.
  • • Damage — finishers once openings exist
  • • Leader — champion whose leader skill is active
Raids Attack & Defense are separate jobs

Attack and Defense can be the same five champions or two completely different teams. Build the best synergy you can first — then swap pieces based on who you face. These are the shapes to invest toward, not “your five highest-power champs.” Roster picks: Team Builder → Raid Attack / Defense.

Attack A — Free Cities speed

Faction: Free Cities stamina engine. Highest-ceiling offense.

  1. Leader: Khal Drogo
  2. Daenerys Targaryen (Blue)
  3. Daario Naharis or Nymeria Sand
  4. Jorah Mormont or Olenna Tyrell
  5. Second Dany / Gregor / Nymeria

Why: Drogo lead starts Free Cities with huge stamina. Skills dump before the defense stabilizes. Do not bench Drogo + Blue Dany.

Attack B — Dual Dany + body

High-investment Targ / Free Cities hybrid seen on top boards.

  1. Leader: Sandor Clegane or Arya
  2. Daenerys Targaryen (Blue)
  3. Daenerys Targaryen (Yellow / Khaleesi)
  4. Jorah Mormont
  5. Arya Stark or Nymeria

Why: Two invested Danys carry damage while Sandor / Jorah keep them alive. Beats raw-power stacks when the board cooperates.

Defense A — Meryn / Lannister hold

Faction: Lannister + Taunt. Hardest common hold to break.

  1. Leader: Meryn Trant
  2. Sandor Clegane
  3. Tywin Lannister or Cersei
  4. Olenna Tyrell or Nymeria
  5. Joffrey / Tyrion / Stannis

Why: Meryn punishes every enemy skill with Stun + Wound. Attacker controls gems, so defense needs punish loops, not a race. Keep Meryn alive.

Defense B — Olenna + Nymeria wall

Control duo that currently sits on a lot of high-end boards.

  1. Leader: Tywin / Meryn / Cersei
  2. Olenna Tyrell
  3. Nymeria Sand
  4. Gregor Clegane
  5. Margaery / Cersei / Jaqen

Why: Olenna + Nymeria stall and strip while Gregor / Lannisters soak. Strong vs skill-spam attack teams. Weaker if the attacker never skills into the punish.

Matchup note: a strong Stark-led wall can lose to Free Cities with Varys lead (Scout + Stealth + Gem Crit). Do not assume the highest-power defense wins.

War 4 teams, 4 jobs, no duplicates

Stage 2 locks teams. Stage 3 is outpost placement (bonuses apply). Stage 4 carries those bonuses into the final. Sometimes you chase the outpost faction bonus. Sometimes you just field the strongest synergy and ignore a weak bonus. Never pad a camp with an underleveled champion just to “count” the faction. Roster builder: Team Builder → Alliance War.

How to pick between bonus vs raw synergy
  • Baratheon War Camp (+25% ATK to Baratheons) — put 3–4 real Baratheons on that outpost. Do not use Robert / Barristan if they are far behind the rest of the team.
  • Stone Keep / Barricade / Maester’s Sigil — generic DEF / Tenacity / Unnatural Res. Any strong team can sit there; prefer a hold team.
  • If the bonus is small and your faction pieces are weak, skip it. A 1M synergy team beats a 850k “bonus” team that is missing its engine.

Four proven war shapes (aim these — then swap to your roster)

TEAM 1 — Lannister core

Faction: Lannister. Best default attack or hybrid hold.

Tywin (Leader) · Gregor · Cersei · Jaime / Tyland / Tyrion · Jaqen / Sandor

Why: Lannister DEF + Treasury + Mountain pressure is the most common high-end shape. Use vs unknown or tanky defenses. Do not default Daemon here.

TEAM 2 — Free Cities speed

Faction: Free Cities. Highest-ceiling offense.

Drogo (Leader) · Daenerys (Blue) · Daario · Jorah / Nymeria · second Dany or Olenna

Why: Stamina loop ends fights before holds stabilize. Keep Drogo + Blue Dany together. Place on a generic outpost or vs slow walls.

TEAM 3 — Baratheon camp / Scout

Faction: Baratheon when the camp is up. Else Varys Scout snowball.

Davos or Varys (Leader) · Stannis · Joffrey · Brienne · Melisandre / Gendry

Why: On Baratheon Camp this is the bonus team — 3–4 Baratheons + one high-power filler. Off-camp, Varys + Davos Scout/Crit can delete Stark walls that look stronger on paper.

TEAM 4 — Meryn hold

Job: sit on a DEF / Tenacity outpost and refuse to die.

Meryn (Leader) · Sandor / Areo · Olenna · Nymeria · Ned / Tyrion

Why: Defense loses more because the attacker owns the gems. Meryn + Olenna/Nymeria force the attacker to skill into Stun/Wound. Keep the Taunter alive.

If you only have one strong team, build Team 2 or Team 1 first. Then add the hold. Four complete mid teams beat three perfect ones and an empty fourth. Composition beats total might.

Dragons Boss Counters

Full notes: Dragon Bosses page · Roster picks: Team Builder.

Quick match: Viserion → Raid + Brittle · Drogon → speed · Rhaegal → Bleed/Fire/Poison · Icy → Reinforce care
Viserion notes: Do not hit while Pacified. Every Raid stack helps. Wake destroys Shields. Immune to Fire/Poison. Reinforces accelerate Patience — be careful.
Tips playbook

• Spread Raid and Taunt across War teams — don’t put every key tool on Team 1.

• Four complete teams beat three perfect ones and one empty shell.

• Chase the outpost faction bonus only when the champions on that team are actually strong. A weak Robert does not belong on Baratheon Camp.

• Matchups matter: Varys Free Cities can delete a “stronger” Stark wall. Lannister/Meryn holds punish skill spam.

• These shapes are investment targets for the alliance — build toward them even if you cannot field the full version yet.

• Power numbers do not decide fights. Composition and status synergies matter more than total might.
