How to use this site: Open the sections below as needed. Sample teams list a Leader first. Swap any name for the strongest champion you own in that role. See Champions for more options.
Quick start (60 seconds)
  1. Pick your strongest 5 for a primary offense team
  2. Build a Taunt + sustain team next
  3. Put remaining Raid + damage on a third team
  4. Fill a fourth team with leftover strong pieces
  5. For dragons: always bring Raid, then adjust for that boss
Decision flow
  • • Raiding? Open Raids → use Attack or Defense samples
  • • War night? Use the 4 War samples (no overlapping names), then place as leadership asks
  • • Dragon? Open Dragons → match the boss sample
  • • Missing a name? Swap using the role notes on Champions
Core principles
  • • Roles first, names second. Cover Raid, damage, and sustain/control.
  • • Spread key tools. Don’t stack every Taunt and Raid applier on one team.
  • • Leaders matter. Strong leader skills belong in the leader slot.
  • • Complete teams beat incomplete theory. Four full teams beat three perfect ones and one weak shell.
Raids Attack & Defense

Sample — Raid Attack

Leader first · swap freely

  1. Leader: Euron Greyjoy (Raid / pressure)
  2. Daemon (damage)
  3. Dragon champion (damage / pressure)
  4. Jon Snow (utility)
  5. Ned Stark (control / utility)

Swaps: Any strong Raid applier for Euron · any high damage for Daemon/dragon · any clean control for Ned.

Sample — Raid Defense

Leader first · swap freely

  1. Leader: Brienne of Tarth (Taunt / sustain)
  2. Corlys Velaryon (sustain / support)
  3. Night King (control / pressure)
  4. Jon Snow (utility)
  5. Flex damage or buffer

Swaps: Any Taunt tank for Brienne · any durable support for Corlys · any control for Night King.

Attack vs Defense
  • Attack: Raid first, damage second, control third
  • Defense: Taunt first, sustain second, punish tools third
Role skeletons:
  • • Attack: Raid · Damage · Damage/Control · Utility · Support
  • • Defense: Taunt · Sustain · Counter/Control · Flex · Buffer
Attack checklist: Consistent Raid? Real damage? Any control? Not over-reliant on Shields?
War 4 Non-Overlapping Teams

Four sample teams with no shared champions. Swap within roles if you don’t own a name.

TEAM 1 — PRIMARY OFFENSE
  1. Leader: Daemon
  2. Dragon champion (damage)
  3. Jon Snow
  4. Utility / support flex
  5. Second damage or control

Your strongest synergy core.

TEAM 2 — CONTROL / SUSTAIN
  1. Leader: Brienne of Tarth
  2. Corlys Velaryon
  3. Ned Stark
  4. Flex sustain or control
  5. Flex damage

Hard to kill; holds pressure.

TEAM 3 — RAID / BURST
  1. Leader: Euron Greyjoy
  2. Second damage dealer
  3. Control / strip piece
  4. Pressure flex
  5. Support or secondary Raid

Cracks defensive setups.

TEAM 4 — FLEX / SPECIALIST
  1. Leader: Night King (or best remaining leader)
  2. Mance Rayder
  3. Remaining strong pieces
  4. Epic/common fillers as needed
  5. Keep a clear purpose

Still a real team, not empty leftovers.

Building order: Strongest 5 → Team 1 · Taunt/sustain → Team 2 · Raid+damage → Team 3 · leftovers → Team 4
Placement: Follow alliance leadership on outposts. Multiple teams can share an outpost if that is the plan. Team quality first, bonuses second.
War checklist: 4 complete teams · Taunt/Raid spread · clear purpose per team · best leaders in leader slots
Dragons Boss Counters
V

Viserion – sample team

Raid + Brittle + single-target · light on Shields/Reinforce
  1. Leader: Euron Greyjoy (Raid)
  2. Daemon or dragon damage
  3. Brittle applicator (if available)
  4. Jon Snow or Ned utility
  5. Flex damage / control

Do not hit while Pacified. Brittle = safe while sleeping. Avoid pure Shield walls.

Drogon – direction

Speed + pressure. Birthright if you have it.

Leader: best speed/pressure piece · add Raid · skip pure tanks

Rhaegal – direction

Bleed + Fire + protect Taunter.

Leader: Taunt/sustain · Bleed appliers · skill-every-turn pressure

Icy Viserion – direction

Reinforce value + skill timing.

Leader: Night King–style Reinforce · control skill cadence

Quick matching: Viserion → Raid+Brittle · Drogon → speed · Rhaegal → Bleed/Fire · Icy → Reinforce
Dragon checklist: Right boss tools? Real damage? Not over-Shielded?
