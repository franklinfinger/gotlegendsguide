Starter path (new to the guide)
  1. Open Champions and note your best Raid, Taunt, and damage pieces
  2. Build War Set A using those roles (swap freely)
  3. For Raids, start with Attack A + Defense A
  4. For dragons, match the boss rules in the Dragons section
  5. Use the Tips playbook when something feels stuck
Role glossary (one line each)
  • • Raid — stacks that weaken the enemy; skill-based is preferred
  • • Taunt — forces the enemy to hit the right target
  • • Sustain — heals, shields, or durability that keeps the team up
  • • Control — stun, strip, pacify, reinforce, ice, or other fight-shaping tools
  • • Damage — finishers once openings exist
  • • Leader — the champion whose leader skill is active for the team
Before you lock teams — 30-second checklist
  • At least two teams with Raid or real pressure?
  • Taunt / sustain on more than one team?
  • Every team complete with a clear job?
  • Best leaders in leader slots?
  • Key tools not all stacked on Team 1?

If the enemy defense looks like…
  • Heavy Taunt / sustain → Raid + burst (Attack A or C)
  • Pure Shield wall → strip / pressure; avoid more Shields
  • Glass cannon → Defense A or C to outlast
  • Reinforce engine → control timing; don’t feed free value

Where to place teams
  • Follow alliance leadership first
  • Strongest team where the fight is hardest
  • Hold teams on contested / defensive outposts
  • Raid/burst into known tanky defenses
  • Don’t gut a team for a small outpost bonus
Dragons Boss Counters

Viserion – Growing Terror

  • • Do not hit while Pacified
  • • Brittle = safe while sleeping
  • • Every Raid stack helps
  • • Wake destroys Shields + refills Stamina
  • • Immune to Fire and Poison
  • • Reinforces accelerate Patience meter

Tips playbook Practical habits
Weekly alliance rhythm
  1. War day — build 4 teams early
  2. Read leadership outpost plan before placing
  3. Run dragons with the right tools
  4. Update one team at a time when you pull a new legendary
Common mistakes
  • All Raid on one team, zero on the others
  • Three strong teams + one empty shell
  • Ignoring leader skills
  • Pure Shield walls into bosses that erase them
  • Copying a sample without swapping for what you own
Swap matrix
  • Need Raid → Euron or skill Raid dragon
  • Need Taunt → Brienne, Meryn, Sandor
  • Need damage → Daemon, Gregor, dragons, Arya
  • Need heal → Cersei, Laenor, Corlys-style sustain
  • Need control → Night King, Ned, Jon, Tywin, Jaqen
If your roster is smaller

Still aim for four complete War teams. Use epics for Taunt, cleanse, and Team 4. Clear roles beat incomplete “perfect” lineups.

After a loss — quick review
  • Did Raid land before you needed damage?
  • Did Taunt hold, or did the wrong target die first?
  • Were Shields erased and left you empty?
  • Was the leader skill actually useful that fight?
