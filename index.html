V

Viserion – Growing Terror

Key mechanics already processed
Critical Rules
  • • Do not hit while Pacified
  • • Brittle = safe to hit while sleeping
  • • Every Raid stack significantly weakens him
  • • He destroys Shields on wake + refills Stamina
  • • Immune to Fire & Poison
Team Direction

Prioritize:

  • • Reliable Raid application (from Skills preferred)
  • • High single-target pressure
  • • Ability to apply Brittle
  • • Avoid pure Shield walls

Drogon

Fast champs to stop Brimstone Rain. Birthright strong. Immune to Fire. Destroys shields.

Rhaegal

Skill every turn → Bleed excellent. Fire bonus damage. Keep Taunter alive.

Icy Viserion

Reinforce punishes him. Strong gem teams. Frequent Skills trigger Icebound sooner. Immune to most debuffs.
