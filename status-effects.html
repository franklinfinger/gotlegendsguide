Status Effects

Definitions from in-game tooltips shared by the alliance.

Wound

Blocks healing / recovery on the target. Core to Lannister pressure (Gregor, Tywin lines).

Fire

Damage over time / Fire gems. Strong with Targaryen and dragon lines.

Ice / Brittle

Ice leads into Brittle (double damage windows). Key for Viserion / Icy fights.

Raid

Stacks that weaken the enemy. Euron is the primary skill-Raid leader.

Taunt

Forces the enemy to hit the right target. Brienne, Sandor, Barristan, Jorah.

Poison

Damage over time / control. Olenna, Oberyn, Nymeria tools.

Ruin

Stacks that cut max HP (Tywin). Confirmed from in-game tooltips.

Anger

Joffrey line — ties into Treasury triggers.

Treasury

Lannister economy / skill fuel.

Fury / Enraged

Rhaenyra / Rhaenys / Daemon fire pressure lines.

Reinforce

Night King / Ice Warden engines. Careful use on Icy Viserion.

True Damage

Bypasses normal defenses (Jaqen, Gwayne tools).

More exact tooltip text is added as screenshots come in.
