Status Effects Glossary

Exact definitions from in-game tooltips. Understanding these is the difference between good teams and great ones.

Core Damage & Defense

Physical Damage — Standard attack damage reduced by Physical Resistance.
True Damage — Ignores all modifiers, resistances, and basic DEF. Very rare and powerful.
Unnatural Damage — Damage type used by many White Walker / Ice effects. Reduced by Unnatural Resistance.
Fire Damage — Reduced by Fire Resistance. Often applies the FIRE status.
Physical Resistance — % reduction to incoming Physical Damage. Max Buff +80%, Min Debuff –90%.
Fire Resistance — % reduction to incoming Fire Damage. Max Buff +80%, Min Debuff –90%.
Unnatural Resistance — % reduction to Unnatural Damage. Same caps as other resistances.
HP Pool — A champion’s Current HP plus any Shield amount.
HP Guard — Protects a champion from damage that would take them below a threshold (e.g. 10% or 25%). Limited triggers.
Shield — Temporary HP that absorbs damage before real HP is touched.
Counter — Reflects incoming damage (usually non-basic-attack) back at the attacker.

Key Debuffs

Wound — Prevents healing. Critical for shutting down sustain teams.
Bleed — Stacking: –5% ATK per stack. Bleeding targets also take damage based on their ATK. Often triggers ally ATK buffs.
Poison — Gem Damage reduced 90%. 5% Max HP Unnatural Damage per turn.
Fire — DoT and enabler for many Targaryen / dragon effects. Multiple variants exist (Roaring, Crippling, Enduring).
Ice — Reduces stamina gain by 5% per stack. Boosts Brittle damage by 20% per stack.
Brittle — Applied at 5 Ice. Target takes 2× damage from skills and traits. Cannot be removed.
Raid — Stacking mark. Each stack reduces enemy ATK by –5%. When a Raided enemy is defeated, effects often spread (Fear, more Raid, etc.).
Fear — Delays the target’s basic attack by 1 turn per stack. Also causes Unnatural Damage strikes in some kits.
Stun — Prevents stamina gain and actions for the duration.
Pacify — Target cannot deal damage or use abilities until hit by a skill.
Deceive — Prevents the target from using their Skill.
Scout / Scouted — Reveals or marks the target, often enabling extra damage or follow-up effects.
Ruin — Tywin specialty. At 3 stacks: cuts Current HP by 75% and applies –25% DEF (non-dragon targets).

Key Buffs

Stealth — Cannot be targeted by most enemy abilities. Often starts with duration (e.g. 10 turns) and grants stacking ATK while active.
Taunt — Forces enemies to attack this champion. Core defensive tool.
Fury — Stacking resource. At threshold often becomes Enraged.
Enraged — 50% less damage taken (common version). Many leaders trigger powerful effects when any ally becomes Enraged.
Warmonger — +5% ATK and +5% Crit Chance per stack. Often gained on ally defeat.
Birthright — Powerful stacking buff used by several leaders (Tyland, Sunfyre, etc.).
Anger — Joffrey specialty. Gains stacks when enemies use Skills. Extra Physical Damage per stack on his skill if Treasury is paid.
Treasury / Coins — Resource for certain skills and iconic abilities (Tywin, Gregor, Joffrey, etc.).
Renew — Healing or cleanse-related buff that appears in several ally gems and skills.
Inspire — Usually grants stamina or damage bonuses.

Other Important Mechanics

Tenacity — Helps resist incoming Debuffs and Buff removal. Chance to land is determined by attacker Hit vs defender Tenacity.
Dodge Chance — Chance to avoid an attack. Determined by attacker Accuracy vs defender Dodge.
Critical Damage / Gem Crit Chance — Multipliers on critical hits and gem matches.
Reinforce — Champions that enter the battle via revive or other abilities. Many kits trigger on Reinforce.
Bide — Blocks stamina gain and actions until enough turns/actions are completed.
Sustained — Effect that repeats every turn.
Resurgence — Grants Birthright stacks + fills stamina (Sunfyre specialty).
Golden Bastion — +35% DEF that refreshes under certain conditions.
Predator’s Gaze — Marks strongest enemy with –10% Dodge / Tenacity / ATK.
Blood Debt — –25% Accuracy & Tenacity, –10% Stamina Rate to the enemy Leader. Unremovable (Oberyn specialty).
Viper’s Bite — +5% Dodge per stack (Oberyn).

These definitions come directly from in-game tooltips provided by the alliance. They will be expanded as more precise wording is captured.

Understanding how these interact (Ice → Brittle, Raid stacking, Fury → Enraged, Wound shutting down heals, etc.) is what makes strong teams.
