Faction Bonuses

Every full (or near-full) faction team grants a powerful global bonus. These are real and strong — but the best teams often mix factions when the status interactions are better.

Rule of thumb: Pure 5-man faction teams are excellent when you have the investment. Hybrid teams win when Ice/Brittle, Fire stacking, Raid pressure, Fury chains, or specific Leader skills matter more than the +25% Gem Damage.

Baratheon (x5)

All team members gain +25% Gem Damage and +30% ATK.

Blacks (x5 / x3)

All team members gain +25% Gem Damage and +30% DEF.

Brotherhood (x4)

All team members gain +25% Gem Damage and +30% Critical Strike Chance.

Ally Bonus (The Brotherhood): Heals the weakest member by 5% Max HP.

Free Cities (x5)

All team members gain +25% Gem Damage and have the Stamina cost of their Skill reduced by 35%.

Free Folk (x5)

All team members gain +25% Gem Damage and +30% Critical Strike Chance.

Greens (x5)

All team members gain +25% Gem Damage and +25% Power.

Greyjoy (x5)

All team members gain +25% Gem Damage and have the Stamina cost of their Skill reduced by 25%.

Lannister (x5)

All team members gain +25% Gem Damage and +30% DEF.

Night’s Watch (x5)

All team members gain +25% Gem Damage and +25% ATK.

Ally Bonus (Southern Crows): Grants the weakest ally a 5% HP Pool Shield.

Stark (x5)

All team members gain +25% Gem Damage and +30% DEF.

Targaryen (x5)

All team members gain +25% Gem Damage and +25% Durability.

Faction bonuses scale with the number of matching champions (x3 / x4 / x5). Exact thresholds can vary slightly by faction.

Leader skills and Ally gems sit on top of these bonuses and are often the real reason a team wins.
