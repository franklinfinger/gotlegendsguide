Faction Bonuses

Full (or near-full) faction teams grant a global bonus. Hybrid teams still win when status interactions matter more than the +25% Gem Damage. Champion lists below help you see who counts for each faction.

Rule of thumb: Pure 5-man faction teams are excellent when you have the investment. Hybrid teams win when Ice/Brittle, Fire stacking, Raid pressure, Fury chains, or specific Leader skills matter more than the faction bonus.

Baratheon (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +30% ATK.

Champions: Davos Seaworth, Gendry, Joffrey Baratheon, Melisandre, Robert Baratheon, Stannis Baratheon

Blacks (x5 / x3)

+25% Gem Damage and +30% DEF.

Champions: Rhaenyra Targaryen, Rhaenys Targaryen, Daemon Targaryen, Jacaerys Velaryon, Laenor Velaryon, Corlys Velaryon (and related Blacks-aligned pieces)

Brotherhood (x4)

+25% Gem Damage and +30% Critical Strike Chance.

Ally Bonus: Heals weakest member by 5% Max HP.

Champions: Beric Dondarrion, Thoros of Myr

Free Cities (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and 35% Skill stamina cost reduction.

Champions: Khal Drogo, Daario Naharis, Craghas Drahar

Free Folk (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +30% Critical Strike Chance.

Champions: Mance Rayder, Tormund Giantsbane, Wun Wun, Ygritte

Greens (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +25% Power.

Champions: Aegon II Targaryen, Aemond Targaryen, Alicent Hightower, Criston Cole, Otto Hightower, Gwayne Hightower (Greens-aligned)

Greyjoy (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and 25% Skill stamina cost reduction.

Champions: Euron Greyjoy, Theon Greyjoy, Yara Greyjoy

Lannister (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +30% DEF.

Champions: Cersei Lannister, Gregor Clegane, Jaime Lannister, Joffrey Baratheon, Sandor Clegane, Tyrion Lannister, Tywin Lannister, Bronn, Tyland Lannister

Night’s Watch (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +25% ATK.

Ally Bonus: 5% HP Pool Shield to weakest ally.

Champions: Benjen Stark, Samwell Tarly, Yoren

Stark (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +30% DEF.

Champions: Arya Stark, Benjen Stark, Bran Stark, Brienne of Tarth, Catelyn Stark, Ghost, Jon Snow, Ned Stark, Robb Stark, Sansa Stark, Talisa Stark

Targaryen (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +25% Durability.

Champions: Daenerys Targaryen, Daemon Targaryen, Rhaenyra Targaryen, Rhaenys Targaryen, Viserys Targaryen, Aegon II, Aemond, Egg, Jorah Mormont, Grey Worm, Barristan Selmy, Meleys, Caraxes, Sunfyre, Adolescent dragons, Icy Viserion

Martell

No global x5 bonus listed in current data — still strong for Taunt / Bleed packages.

Champions: Areo Hotah, Nymeria Sand, Oberyn Martell

Tyrell

Support / control pieces; often used in hybrid teams.

Champions: Margaery Tyrell, Olenna Tyrell

White Walker / Others

Ice and special tools.

Champions: The Night King, Ice Warden, Icy Viserion · Also: Lord Varys, Ser Duncan the Tall, Jaqen H'ghar, etc.

Faction bonuses scale with matching champions (x3 / x4 / x5). Leader skills and Ally gems often matter more than the bonus alone. Lists are based on the current champion library — more will be added as the roster expands.
