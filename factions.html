Faction Bonuses

Legendary champions only, from alliance roster screenshots. Same-name champions are labeled by gem color or subtitle. Champions can appear on more than one list (see Dual Faction).

Rule of thumb: Pure faction teams are strong when you have the investment. Hybrid teams win when status interactions (Raid, Bleed, Ice/Brittle, Taunt) matter more than the faction bonus.

Baratheon

+25% Gem Damage and +30% ATK.

Champions (11): Davos Seaworth, Sandor Clegane, Stannis Baratheon (Red), Joffrey Baratheon, Brienne of Tarth, Lord Varys, Melisandre, Gendry, Stannis Baratheon (Purple), Barristan Selmy, Robert Baratheon

Blacks

+25% Gem Damage and +30% DEF.

Champions (8): Rhaenys Targaryen, Rhaenyra Targaryen, Meleys, Laenor Velaryon, Jacaerys Velaryon, Daemon Targaryen, Corlys Velaryon, Caraxes

Bolton

Bolton-aligned Legendary only.

Champions (3): Roose Bolton, Ramsay Bolton, Walder Frey

Brotherhood

+25% Gem Damage and +30% Critical Strike Chance. Ally Bonus: Heals weakest member by 5% Max HP.

Champions (4): Arya Stark, Gendry, Beric Dondarrion, Thoros of Myr

Free Cities

+25% Gem Damage and 35% Skill stamina cost reduction.

Champions (11): Daenerys Targaryen (Blue), Khal Drogo, Daario Naharis, Daenerys Targaryen (Yellow) — Khaleesi of the Great Grass Sea, Adolescent Viserion, Adolescent Rhaegal, Adolescent Drogon, Jorah Mormont, Viserys Targaryen III, Lord Varys, Thoros of Myr

Free Folk

+25% Gem Damage and +30% Critical Strike Chance.

Champions (6): Mance Rayder, Ygritte, Tormund Giantsbane, Craster, Osha, Wun Wun

Greens

+25% Gem Damage and +25% Power. (Hightowers are listed under Greens.)

Champions (8): Gwayne Hightower, Sunfyre, Tyland Lannister, Alicent Hightower, Aemond Targaryen, Aegon II Targaryen, Criston Cole, Otto Hightower
