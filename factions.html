Faction Bonuses

Every full (or near-full) faction team grants a powerful global bonus. These are real and strong — but the best teams often mix factions when the status interactions are better.

Rule of thumb: Pure 5-man faction teams are excellent when you have the investment. Hybrid teams win when Ice/Brittle, Fire stacking, Raid pressure, Fury chains, or specific Leader skills matter more than the +25% Gem Damage.

Baratheon (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +30% ATK.

Blacks (x5 / x3)

+25% Gem Damage and +30% DEF.

Brotherhood (x4)

+25% Gem Damage and +30% Critical Strike Chance.

Ally Bonus: Heals weakest member by 5% Max HP.

Free Cities (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and 35% Skill stamina cost reduction.

Free Folk (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +30% Critical Strike Chance.

Greens (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +25% Power.

Greyjoy (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and 25% Skill stamina cost reduction.

Lannister (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +30% DEF.

Night’s Watch (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +25% ATK.

Ally Bonus: 5% HP Pool Shield to weakest ally.

Stark (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +30% DEF.

Targaryen (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +25% Durability.

Faction bonuses scale with matching champions (x3 / x4 / x5). Leader skills and Ally gems often matter more than the bonus alone.
