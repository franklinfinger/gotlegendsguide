Faction Bonuses

Full (or near-full) faction teams grant a global bonus. Hybrid teams still win when status interactions matter more than the +25% Gem Damage.

Baratheon (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +30% ATK.

Blacks (x5 / x3)

+25% Gem Damage and +30% DEF.

Brotherhood (x4)

+25% Gem Damage and +30% Critical Strike Chance.

Ally Bonus: Heals weakest member by 5% Max HP.

Free Cities (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and 35% Skill stamina cost reduction.

Free Folk (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +30% Critical Strike Chance.

Greens (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +25% Power.

Greyjoy (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and 25% Skill stamina cost reduction.

Lannister (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +30% DEF.

Night’s Watch (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +25% ATK.

Ally Bonus: 5% HP Pool Shield to weakest ally.

Stark (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +30% DEF.

Targaryen (x5)

+25% Gem Damage and +25% Durability.

Faction bonuses scale with matching champions (x3 / x4 / x5). Leader skills and Ally gems often matter more than the bonus alone.
