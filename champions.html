Champions

Role groupings and roster notes based on champions we’ve processed so far. Use these as role guides — not forced team lists. Build with the strongest versions you own.

How to use this page: Cover the jobs first (Raid, Taunt/Sustain, Control, Damage). Then fill with the best champions you have in those roles. Epic and common champions can still be useful fillers when legendaries are short.

Build priority (simple tiers)

TIER A
Cover these first

At least one reliable Raid applier, one real damage threat, and one Taunt/sustain piece.

TIER B
Then add

Control/utility, secondary damage, and leaders that lift whole teams.

TIER C
Fillers

Epics/commons for cleanse, specific debuffs, or to complete a fourth War team.

Role Groups

Taunt / Sustain

Keep the team alive and force the enemy to hit the right target.

  • • Brienne of Tarth — strong Taunt / sustain style kit
  • • Corlys Velaryon — durable, team-support lean

Raid / Pressure

Apply Raid stacks and keep pressure on the enemy.

  • • Euron Greyjoy — Raid / pressure oriented
  • • Certain dragon champions — useful Raid or pressure tools

Control / Utility

Stun, strip, reinforce, ice pressure, and other tools that shape the fight.

  • • Night King — reinforce + ice pressure
  • • Ned Stark — utility / control lean
  • • Jon Snow — flexible utility / pressure
  • • Mance Rayder — utility option

Damage

Finish fights once Raid or openings are in place.

  • • Adolescent dragons — high single-target potential
  • • Daemon — strong damage option
  • • Other high-attack legendaries you have invested in

Simple pairing ideas

RAID ATTACK CORE

Raid applier + 1–2 damage dealers + optional control.

RAID DEFENSE / WAR HOLD

Taunt + sustain + punish tools (counter, shield-break, or control).

DRAGON FLEX

Raid almost always helps. Then add Brittle / Bleed / speed / Reinforce for that boss.

Where each role fits

  • • Taunt / Sustain → Raid Defense, War Team 2, long fights
  • • Raid / Pressure → Raid Attack, War Team 3, most dragons
  • • Control / Utility → Flex into any team that needs clean fights
  • • Damage → Pair with Raid; finishers on Team 1 and Team 3

Best content fits

Raids

Attack needs Raid + damage. Defense needs Taunt + sustain.

War

Spread Taunt and Raid across four teams. Leaders in leader slots.

Viserion

Raid + Brittle + single-target. Careful with Shields and Reinforce.

Other dragons

Drogon: speed. Rhaegal: Bleed/Fire. Icy Viserion: Reinforce + skill timing.

Starter champion notes

Brienne of Tarth

Taunt / sustain. Strong for Raid Defense and War Team 2.

Corlys Velaryon

Durable support lean. Pairs with Taunt cores.

Euron Greyjoy

Raid / pressure. Good for Raid Attack and War Team 3.

Night King

Reinforce + ice pressure. Watch Reinforce into Viserion.

Jon Snow

Flexible utility / pressure across Raid and War.

Ned Stark

Utility / control. Supports cleaner fights.

Daemon

Damage option. Pair with Raid appliers.

Dragon champions

Single-target or pressure. Check kits for Raid, Fire, Bleed, or speed.

Mance Rayder

Utility option. Flex into control/support needs.
