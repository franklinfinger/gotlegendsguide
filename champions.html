Champions

Role groupings and roster notes based on the champions we’ve processed so far. This page will grow as more kit data is locked in. Use these as role guides — not forced team lists.

How to use this page: Pick champions that cover the roles you need (Raid, Taunt/Sustain, Control, Damage). Exact names matter less than covering the jobs with the strongest versions you own.

Taunt / Sustain

Keep the team alive and force the enemy to hit the right target.

  • • Brienne of Tarth — strong Taunt / sustain style kit
  • • Corlys Velaryon — durable, team-support lean

Add more as kit data is confirmed.

Raid / Pressure

Apply Raid stacks and keep pressure on the enemy.

  • • Euron Greyjoy — Raid / pressure oriented
  • • Certain dragon champions — useful Raid or pressure tools

Skill-based Raid is usually preferred when available.

Control / Utility

Stun, strip, reinforce, ice pressure, and other tools that shape the fight.

  • • Night King — reinforce + ice pressure
  • • Ned Stark — utility / control lean
  • • Jon Snow — flexible utility / pressure

Damage

Finish fights once Raid or openings are in place.

  • • Adolescent dragons — high single-target potential
  • • Daemon — strong damage option
  • • Other high-attack legendaries you have invested in

Roster status

We have processed a large portion of the user’s champion screenshots (skills, traits, iconic items where provided). Full named team lists will be filled in as more kits are locked and cross-checked.

  • • Role examples above are based on kits already reviewed
  • • Epic and common champions can still be useful fillers — especially for Taunt, cleanse, or specific debuffs
  • • Gold-outline legendaries are the usual core when fully built
