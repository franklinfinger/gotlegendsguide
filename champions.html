Raids War Dragons Champions Notes

Champions

Role groupings and roster notes based on champions we’ve processed so far. Use these as role guides — not forced team lists. Build with the strongest versions you own.

How to use this page: Cover the jobs first (Raid, Taunt/Sustain, Control, Damage). Then fill with the best champions you have in those roles. Epic and common champions can still be useful fillers when legendaries are short.

Role Groups

Taunt / Sustain

Keep the team alive and force the enemy to hit the right target. Core for Raid Defense and War Team 2.

  • • Brienne of Tarth — strong Taunt / sustain style kit
  • • Corlys Velaryon — durable, team-support lean

Look for other tanks/supports with Taunt, heal, or shield support as you expand the roster.

Raid / Pressure

Apply Raid stacks and keep pressure on the enemy. Core for Raid Attack and War Team 3.

  • • Euron Greyjoy — Raid / pressure oriented
  • • Certain dragon champions — useful Raid or pressure tools

Skill-based Raid is usually preferred when available.

Control / Utility

Stun, strip, reinforce, ice pressure, and other tools that shape the fight.

  • • Night King — reinforce + ice pressure
  • • Ned Stark — utility / control lean
  • • Jon Snow — flexible utility / pressure
  • • Mance Rayder — utility option (data processed)

Damage

Finish fights once Raid or openings are in place.

  • • Adolescent dragons — high single-target potential
  • • Daemon — strong damage option
  • • Other high-attack legendaries you have invested in

Simple pairing ideas

RAID ATTACK CORE

Raid applier + 1–2 damage dealers + optional control. Keep Shields light if the boss erases them.

RAID DEFENSE / WAR HOLD

Taunt + sustain + something that punishes over-aggression (counter, shield-break, or control).

DRAGON FLEX

Raid almost always helps. Then add Brittle / Bleed / speed / Reinforce based on the specific dragon.

Where each role fits

  • • Taunt / Sustain → Raid Defense, War Team 2, any long fight
  • • Raid / Pressure → Raid Attack, War Team 3, most dragons
  • • Control / Utility → Flex into any team that needs clean fights
  • • Damage → Pair with Raid; primary finishers on Team 1 and Team 3

Starter champion notes

Brienne of Tarth

Taunt / sustain style. Strong candidate for Raid Defense and War Team 2.

Corlys Velaryon

Durable support lean. Pairs well with Taunt cores and defensive War setups.

Euron Greyjoy

Raid / pressure. Good fit for Raid Attack and War Team 3.

Night King

Reinforce + ice pressure. Control / utility; watch Reinforce use into Viserion.

Jon Snow

Flexible utility / pressure. Useful across Raid and War depending on the rest of the team.

Ned Stark

Utility / control lean. Helps clean fights and support stronger damage cores.

Daemon

Damage option. Pair with Raid appliers so openings get converted into real damage.

Dragon champions

Often strong single-target or pressure tools. Check each kit for Raid, Fire, Bleed, or speed value.
