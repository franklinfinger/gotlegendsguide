Raids War Dragons Champions Notes

Champions

Expanded roster with role tags and stylized portrait markers. Use these as role guides — swap in the strongest version you own. Personal game screenshots are not published here (keeps your stats private).

How to use: Match roles from the sample teams. If you don’t have a listed name, pick anyone in the same role group with similar tools (Raid, Taunt, control, damage).

Build priority

TIER A — COVER FIRST

Raid applier · real damage · Taunt / sustain

TIER B — THEN ADD

Control / utility · secondary damage · strong leaders

TIER C — FILLERS

Epics / commons for cleanse, debuffs, completing War Team 4
