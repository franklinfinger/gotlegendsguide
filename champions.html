Champions

Role guides and swap options for the sample teams on the main page. Use the strongest champion you own in each role.

How to use: Main page samples list specific names + a Leader. If you don’t have that champion, pick anyone from the same role group below.

Build priority

TIER A
Cover first

Raid applier · real damage · Taunt/sustain

TIER B
Then add

Control/utility · secondary damage · strong leaders

TIER C
Fillers

Epics/commons for cleanse, debuffs, or completing War Team 4

Role groups & swap lists

Taunt / Sustain

Raid Defense · War Team 2

  • • Brienne of Tarth — primary Taunt example
  • • Corlys Velaryon — durable support
  • • Any other Taunt tank or strong sustain support you own

Raid / Pressure

Raid Attack · War Team 3 · most dragons

  • • Euron Greyjoy — primary Raid example
  • • Dragon champions with Raid or heavy pressure
  • • Any skill-based Raid applier

Control / Utility

Flex into any team that needs clean fights

  • • Night King — Reinforce / ice (careful on Viserion)
  • • Ned Stark — control / utility
  • • Jon Snow — flexible utility
  • • Mance Rayder — utility flex

Damage

Pair with Raid · Team 1 & Team 3 finishers

  • • Daemon
  • • Adolescent / dragon champions
  • • Any high-attack legendary you’ve invested in

Leader candidates

Put strong team-wide leader skills in the leader slot. Examples from current samples:

  • • Euron — Raid Attack / War Team 3 / Viserion-style
  • • Brienne — Raid Defense / War Team 2
  • • Daemon — War Team 1 primary offense
  • • Night King — War Team 4 or Icy Viserion direction

If your best leader is different, use that champion as leader instead.
