# GOT Legends Guide source-to-database completeness audit

## Audit boundary and interpretation

This report was written **before** any database or interface correction in this audit. A matching SQLite/Supabase row count means only that the import copied the SQLite snapshot. It does not establish that the snapshot contains the supplied source corpus. Counts below are lower bounds where source screenshots have not been individually classified. `Unknown` is a real audit result, not zero.

The inspected corpus comprises the current and historical Git repository; 90 legacy champion JSON rows (88 distinct IDs); 93 legacy portrait definitions (83 distinct keys); the local verified SQLite snapshot; three project conversation PDFs; and the connected Google Drive folder containing 833 original images. The SQLite `sources` table holds 620 references. Only five of those refer to files in the connected Drive folder. The other 615 point to earlier ChatGPT uploads or Library references not present as image bytes in Git or the Drive folder. Thus the source inventory contains **at least 1,448 distinct image references/files** (833 + 620 − 5), not 620 validated screenshots. The 833 Drive images have not yet been classified image by image. No screenshot is accepted as a fact by filename alone.

`Complete` and `partial` in the baseline table mean the SQLite row's own review flag, where one exists. They do **not** mean complete against all supplied screenshots. `No image` means no structured subject-to-image relationship in SQLite/Supabase; a legacy UI portrait may still exist. `No provenance` means no direct source/evidence link on that row or association. Counts of unlike units are not added together.

## Exact discrepancies already recoverable from repository sources

### Champion identities and variants

The following 19 distinct legacy functional champion names have no matching SQLite champion identity under their ordinary names: **Aegon II Targaryen, Aemond Targaryen, Baela Targaryen, Balon Greyjoy, Caraxes, Doran Martell, Ellaria Sand, Harwin Strong, Helaena Targaryen, Hizdahr zo Loraq, Illyrio Mopatis, Jeor Mormont, Larys Strong, Loras Tyrell, Theon Greyjoy, Thoros of Myr, Tyland Lannister, Wun Wun, and Yara Greyjoy.** Their legacy records are not automatically accepted as current, screenshot-verified cards. The prior [canonical audit](CANONICAL_DATA_AUDIT.md) also documents generic IDs that conflate variants, including Daenerys, Stannis, Joffrey, Cersei, Tyrion, Jon, Rhaenyra, Viserys, and the playable/encounter dragon distinction.

SQLite champion rows lacking a skill: **Jaqen H'ghar** and **Roose Bolton**. Rows lacking detailed stats: **Lyanna Mormont** and **Walder Frey**. Fifty champion rows have no faction value. `champions.faction` also contains literal icon descriptions and combined labels such as `Free Cities (icon)`, `Lannister (lion icon)`, `Baratheon (stag icon)`, `Blacks and Targaryen`, and `Lannister; Baratheon`; these are not separate faction identities. `Wildling` versus `Free Folk` needs source review rather than an automatic synonym assumption.

The 30 iconic ability rows name 30 distinct items, while the item table has only **ICE** and **Oathkeeper**. The omitted item names are: Blackwater Blade, Burning Blade, CatsPaw Dagger, Chain Of Dragons, Crown Of Cruelty, Dire Wolf Fang, Dragon Brooch, Dragon Eggs, Dragonglass Dagger, Enshrouding Helm, Hardhome Blade, Hatchling Dragons, Lannister Coin Purse, Longclaw, Massive Scorpion, Needle, Old King's Crown, Pouch Of Fingers, Red Queen's Battle Crown, Red Wedding Chalice, Red Woman's Ruby Necklace, Rose Gold Pendant, The Khal's Arakh, The Kingslayer's Blade, Valyrian Steel Necklace, White Cloak, Widow's Wail, and Ygritte's Bow. `ICE`/`Ice` may be the same item but case alone is not sufficient proof; keep both labels traceable.

### Legendary Assault

The legacy guide documents **Viserion, Drogon, Rhaegal, and Icy Viserion** as Legendary Assault encounters. SQLite/Supabase hold only partial **Rhaegal**. Original connected Drive screenshots `IMG_2751.PNG` through `IMG_2759.PNG` visibly establish Drogon, subtitle *The Terror*, and six named abilities: **Dracarys, Aegis Bane, Ember Storm II, Born in Flames II, Daznak's Fury, Brimstone Rain II**. The screenshots show complete continuation text for Ember Storm, Born in Flames, and Brimstone Rain across adjacent images. One legacy PDF incorrectly says the first line of Born in Flames was cut off; `IMG_2755.PNG` shows the immunity list. This is a concrete source/secondary-summary conflict. Viserion and Icy Viserion mechanics remain legacy claims until their original screenshots can be inspected.

### Faction and strategy state

The prior legacy guide lists 14 faction-filter categories, including `No Faction` and `Dual Faction`, which are filters rather than game factions. The SQLite import instead generated 18 faction records directly from distinct literal `champions.faction` strings. This explains the false inflation from icon descriptions and compound labels. Current membership, announced additions, and uncertain legacy classifications must remain separate.

All 15 structured strategy teams have `visible_composition`, with no `outcome_verified` rows. Their membership is stored as unlinked names; no team should be described as a proven victory. The three unsourced Raid rules are `initial_fights_and_duration`, `fight_replenishment`, and `combo_testing`.

## Required correction gates

1. Preserve every original row, source pointer, portrait script, and legacy file. Add normalized relationships and audit states alongside them.
2. Register the 833 Drive images with stable IDs and original URLs. Classify each image before claiming completeness; a filename alone does not reveal subject or text.
3. Recover and inspect the 615 earlier ChatGPT upload bytes or mark each unavailable; do not promote claims based only on metadata.
4. Add candidate legacy identities, items, portraits, Legendary Assault encounters, and abilities with provenance and review states. Only screenshot-supported exact text may be marked verified.
5. Validate item/ability/champion, normalized faction, team-member, portrait, and evidence references before exposing new current guidance.
6. Recount live Supabase independently after a transactionally applied migration. Do not use checksum or row-count agreement as the completeness gate.

## Unresolved evidence scope

This is an evidence-bounded baseline, **not a claim of full source completeness**. Exact expected totals for every ability, item, phase, modifier, faction membership, and image cannot be known until the 833 Drive files and 615 earlier upload references are classified and reconciled. The report will be revised as that work proceeds; unknown values will not be replaced by guessed numbers.

### Exact known unresolved or unrecoverable evidence

The following **11 legacy-only champion candidates** still have no identified original skill or trait cards in the inspected source corpus: **Baela Targaryen, Balon Greyjoy, Doran Martell, Ellaria Sand, Harwin Strong, Helaena Targaryen, Hizdahr zo Loraq, Illyrio Mopatis, Jeor Mormont, Larys Strong, and Loras Tyrell**. The 615 earlier ChatGPT/Library upload references have no available original bytes in the connected Drive folder or repository. The 833 Drive PNGs were all fetched and OCR-indexed, and each file was byte/size checked. **They have not all been visually classified or compared line-by-line to structured records.** Therefore the full source-to-database audit remains incomplete in the strict sense: exact expected totals for every skill, gear piece, modifier, phase, companion, and team combination remain unknown. This is a material limitation, not a count-match success. The record tables are private under RLS, and unverified candidate cards are not exposed as confirmed public guidance. No interface redesign or production deployment was performed.
