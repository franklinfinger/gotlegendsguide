Ally Pairs

Many legendary champions have an Ally partner. The Ally replaces a specific board clear type and grants a unique Gem when used. Allies are helpful — not always game-changing — but the right pairing improves board control and adds free value (heals, stuns, Raid, Fury, etc.).

How to use this page

When you have both partners available, prefer the pair. The Gem effect is the real prize. If you only have one, the champion still works normally.
